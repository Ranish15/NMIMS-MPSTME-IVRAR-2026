## 🎯 Authorized Research Title
> **"To what extent does an immersive VR phishing simulation improve threat recognition and mitigate cognitive bias compared to standard 2D web-based cybersecurity training across a 14-day retention interval?"**

### Academic Standard & Foundation
* **Governing Standard:** Verizon DBIR 2024 (68% of breaches involve human element). Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve shows < 25-30% retention of lecture training after 14 days without experiential immersion.
* **Scientific Scope:** Directly compared VR phishing simulation against standard 2D web-based training (corporate baseline); added explicit 14-day retention interval to model Ebbinghaus forgetting curve decay.
