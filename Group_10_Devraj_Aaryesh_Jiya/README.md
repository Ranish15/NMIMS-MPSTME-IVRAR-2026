## 🎯 Authorized Research Title
> **"How can an OpenCV-based color and fiducial hand-tracking pipeline integrated with Unity VR achieve sub-15ms latency and gesture recognition accuracy for architectural 3D model reviews without dedicated 6-DoF controllers?"**

### Academic Standard & Foundation
* **Governing Standard:** Motion-to-photon latency in VR must remain <= 15-20ms to prevent jitter and dissociation. Fitts' Law spatial object manipulation throughput. HSV thresholding on standard webcams takes < 5ms CPU time.
* **Scientific Scope:** Formulated as an interrogative inquiry; established sub-15ms latency and gesture recognition accuracy as quantifiable engineering targets; anchored to architectural 3D model reviews.
