## 🎯 Authorized Research Title
> **"How can an immersive VR sensory-stress simulation improve target prioritization and reaction latency for defensive security trainees under simulated low-visibility nighttime conditions?"**

### Academic Standard & Foundation
* **Governing Standard:** Hick-Hyman Law & Signal Detection Theory (d'). Under sensory degradation, visual choice reaction time increases from 250ms to 600-900ms. d' = Z(Hit Rate) - Z(False Alarm Rate).
* **Scientific Scope:** Purged the fabricated 31% figure; replaced restricted NDA cadets with defensive security trainees; shifted from sci-fi shooting to an empirical human factors stress simulation measuring target prioritization accuracy (F1 / d') and reaction latency in milliseconds.
