## 🎯 Authorized Research Title
> **"How can an AR visual-marker navigation system using ArUco and QR anchors optimize transit time and route-finding errors across multi-storey university buildings for first-year students?"**

### Academic Standard & Foundation
* **Governing Standard:** Mobile ARCore/ARKit VIO drifts by 1-3% of distance traveled (1-3m error per 100m corridor). Physical ArUco/QR markers ground-truth anchor coordinates, resetting drift to < 5cm. Dijkstra shortest path on 3D node graphs.
* **Scientific Scope:** Refined dependent variables to transit time in seconds and route-finding errors; specified ArUco and QR anchors to correct mobile AR tracking drift.
