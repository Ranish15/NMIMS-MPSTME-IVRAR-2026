## 🎯 Authorized Research Title
> **"How can a hybrid smart AR kiosk and mobile handoff system reduce transit time and paper map waste for campus visitors navigating complex university facilities?"**

### Academic Standard & Foundation
* **Governing Standard:** ISO 14001 Environmental Management. Large campuses distribute 15,000-25,000 paper maps annually, 80% discarded in 24h. Mobile handoff latency must remain < 3.0s to maintain seamless user flow.
* **Scientific Scope:** Integrated the hybrid kiosk-to-mobile QR handoff mechanism (selecting destination on kiosk generates dynamic URL for mobile AR session); established transit time and quantified paper reduction metrics.
